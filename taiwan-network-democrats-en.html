Members and evidence

Criteria. "Taiwan record" = a traceable Taiwan-related bill, vote, public text, or caucus role; it does not establish a current 2026 position. "China stance, no Taiwan" = statements or actions on China trade or security that do not mention Taiwan. "Military restraint, no Taiwan" = advocates cutting military spending, withdrawing troops, or arms embargoes without mentioning Taiwan; this does not equal opposing arms sales to Taiwan. "No record found" = nothing turned up in about one or two searches per person; this does not mean the person has no position.

Factions. Assigned from PAC endorsement lists and media descriptions. They reflect support during the election, not membership in a caucus after taking office. No Welcome, New Dem, or Blue Dog PAC endorsement data was found for incoming senators. "No faction label" includes establishment-backed candidates and those with no faction data found.

Scope. The 13 senators include 2 in uncalled races. The 40 representatives are a selected sample of each faction, not the full list of newly elected members. Some results come from election-day tally pages, and some evidence appears only in search summaries or a single source; check the originals before citing. Translated from the Chinese version.
